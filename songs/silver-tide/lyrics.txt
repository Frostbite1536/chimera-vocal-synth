SILVER TIDE
An original song for 100 CHIMERA voices
E minor · 72 BPM · 4:03

[0:00  Intro: choir, no words]

[0:27  Verse 1]
Wind on the water, across the bay,
grey gulls are turning for home,
light a lantern above the waves,
I will not wander alone.

[0:53  Chorus]
Silver tide, carry me over,
silver tide, carry me home,
far away the shore is calling,
ever on, ever on.

[1:20  Interlude]
Ah… ah…

[1:33  Verse 2]
Stars in the darkness are shining through,
moon on the edge of the sea,
all of the rivers will run to you,
all of the waters to me.

[2:00  Chorus]
Silver tide, carry me over,
silver tide, carry me home,
far away the shore is calling,
ever on, ever on.

[2:27  Bridge]
Lumen maris, lumen stellae,
hold me, the dawn is near.
Lumen maris, lumen stellae,
ah…

[2:53  Final chorus]
Silver tide, carry me over,
silver tide, carry me home,
far away the shore is calling,
ever on, ever on.

[3:20  Tag]
Ever on, ever on,
carry me home.

[3:33  Outro: choir, no words]

—
"Lumen maris, lumen stellae" is invented Latin for "light of the sea, light of the stars."
Under the words, the choir sings "oo" in the verses, "ah" in the choruses and "oh" in the bridge.
